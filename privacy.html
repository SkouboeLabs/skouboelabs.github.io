Dodgie Realms

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026

This policy describes the current version of Dodgie Realms. SkouboeLabs will update it before introducing services that materially change how user data is handled.

1. Introduction

Dodgie Realms is a mobile game developed and published by SkouboeLabs, based in Denmark. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install or use Dodgie Realms.

2. Information We Collect

The current version does not collect, store, transmit or share personal information or required user-data categories. The game does not require an account or login.

SkouboeLabs does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, videos, audio recordings, messages or payment-card details through the current version.

3. Local Game Data

Game progress, high scores, collected stars, unlocked content and settings may be stored locally on your device. This data is used to operate the game and is not transmitted to SkouboeLabs by the current version.

Local data may be removed if you uninstall the game, clear app data or reset your device. SkouboeLabs cannot restore deleted local data.

4. In-App Purchases

Dodgie Realms may offer optional digital purchases through Google Play Billing. Payment processing, credentials, transaction records and refunds are handled by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy practices.

SkouboeLabs does not receive or store full payment-card details. When purchases become available, the game may receive limited purchase information needed to confirm and deliver digital content, such as a product identifier, purchase status or transaction token. This policy and the Data safety declaration will be updated if implemented purchases change the game's data practices.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version does not use third-party advertising, analytics, authentication or cloud-save services.

Future versions may introduce Google Play Billing, crash reporting, analytics, cloud saving, advertising or Google Play Games. SkouboeLabs will review provider practices and update this policy and Google Play declarations before release where required.

6. Advertising

The current version does not display third-party advertisements and does not use an advertising identifier for advertising or tracking.

7. Children's Privacy

Dodgie Realms is designed to be suitable for a broad audience, including children. The current version does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, contact skouboelabs@gmail.com.

8. Data Sharing and Sale

SkouboeLabs does not sell personal data. The current version does not share user data with third parties.

9. Data Security

Although the current version does not collect user data, SkouboeLabs takes privacy and security seriously. If online services are introduced, reasonable technical and organizational safeguards will be used.

10. Your Rights

Because the current version does not create accounts or collect personal information, SkouboeLabs generally has no personal user record to access, correct or delete. You may remove locally stored data through device settings or by uninstalling the game.

Residents of the European Economic Area may contact us regarding rights under applicable data-protection law, including the GDPR.

11. International Availability

Dodgie Realms may be available internationally through Google Play. Future processing of personal data will be handled according to applicable law and described in an updated policy.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, legal requirements or third-party services change. The latest version will be published here with a revised date.
